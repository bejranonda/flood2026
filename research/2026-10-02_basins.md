## Experiment (39 gauges outside the focus area, production backtest)
| variant | 12 h over gate | 24 h | 48 h | star RMSE vs base, median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A production (HII basin name, own-cell rain) | 19/39 | 16/39 | 17/39 | — |
| B 22-basin polygon + same river system | 19 | 16 | 18 | ±0 % vs A (upstream set changed at 1/39) |
| C B + rain of own + upstream gauges' cells | 19 | 17 | 18 | ±0 % vs B (better at 5–8/39) |
| D B + basin-mean rain | 19 | 15 | 17 | ±0 % vs B |
| P B + basin-mean rain of another basin (placebo) | 19 | 15 | 10 | +1.0 / +2.6 / +4.7 % worse |

**Conclusions:** rain location matters (the placebo hurts, 48 h 17→10); the river-system rule is sound and almost neutral (adopted, D-066); basin-mean rain does not help (dilutes over up to ~70,000 km²); the upstream-cells proxy gives a small, not significant gain (not adopted; re-test with HydroBASINS sub-catchments).

## Adopted (v0.17.0, D-066)
1. Every gauge gets `basin22`, `river_main`, `river_system` (weekly `hii_geo`); missing HII basin names are filled ("ลุ่มน้ำ<name>").
2. Learned upstream gauges: same 22-basin polygon, and the same river system when both are on main rivers.
3. "ต้นน้ำ: <gauge> · 24 ชม. ที่ผ่านมา: … · มักถึงที่นี่ในราว N ชม. ⓘ" in the station sheet and the pin panel (measured, never a forecast).
